How MediCheck Works

Three straightforward steps to reduce stress and locate needed medications efficiently.

1

Search for Medicine

Type the medicine brand name, generic salt, or category along with your preferred locality.

2

Compare Reported Prices & Stock

Examine listed rates, check reported inventory badges (In Stock / Low Stock), and see sample travel distance.

3

Call Pharmacy to Confirm

Use the provided direct phone number to confirm current counter availability before travelling.

Project Background

About the MediCheck Project

MediCheck was conceived as an engineering hackathon prototype to address a frequent problem in urban healthcare: the time, cost, and stress patients face visiting multiple pharmacies just to fulfill essential prescriptions.

By aggregating pharmacy-reported availability and pricing data into an intuitive interface, MediCheck enables patients and caregivers to make informed choices, avoid fruitless commutes, and compare costs fairly.

🎓 Student Demonstration Project
Developed for a 1st-Year B.Tech Hackathon. Built completely in HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript with zero external frameworks or backend dependencies.

Demonstration Standards

All sample pharmacy names, distances, prices, and stock figures are simulated for prototyping purposes and do not represent guaranteed real-time inventory.

Important Medical & Data Disclaimer

Medicine prices and availability are demonstration data unless explicitly verified with the pharmacy. Stock may change at any time. Contact the pharmacy before travelling. Follow your healthcare professional’s prescription and advice.
